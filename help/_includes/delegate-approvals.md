# Delegate tasks, issues, and approvals effectively - SHARED

The video explains how to delegate tasks, issues, and approvals. ​ To enable delegation, the system or group administrator must activate the feature in Setup under Project Preferences. ​ Once enabled, users can delegate all their tasks and issues to another person for a specified time period using the "Delegate" button in the My Tasks widget. ​ Delegation applies to both tasks and issues together, and they must be assigned to the same person. ​ Delegations can be set with or without an end date, and users can manually stop delegations at any time. ​
For approvals, users can delegate them via the "Awaiting My Approval" widget. ​ Delegation applies to existing approvals and new ones during the specified time period, but only for projects, tasks, and issues—not for documents, proofs, or timesheets. ​ Notifications for delegated tasks, issues, and approvals can be enabled by the administrator to inform both the delegator and the delegatee. ​
Delegated approvals are marked in the system, showing who delegated them and to whom. ​ Updates related to approvals appear in the Update tab, and system activity can be viewed in the System Activity tab. ​

## Key takeaways

* **Enabling Delegation:** Delegation of tasks, issues, and approvals must first be enabled by the system or group administrator in Setup under Project Preferences.
* **Delegating Tasks and Issues:** Users can delegate all their tasks and issues together (not separately) to a single person for a specified time period via the "Delegate" button in the My Tasks widget. ​ Delegations can be set with or without an end date and can be manually stopped.
* **Delegating Approvals:** Approvals for projects, tasks, and issues can be delegated using the "Delegate Approval" button in the Awaiting My Approval widget. ​ However, approvals for documents, proofs, or timesheets cannot be delegated.
* **Notifications:** Email notifications for delegated tasks, issues, and approvals can be enabled by the administrator to inform both the delegator and the delegatee when delegations are assigned or stopped.
* **Tracking Delegations:** Delegated tasks and approvals are marked in the system, showing who delegated them and to whom. ​ Updates related to approvals appear in the Update tab, and system activity can be viewed in the System Activity tab.
