# Create job roles and rate cards - SHARED

The video explains how to create a job role and a rate card, and how to attach and remove a rate card from a project.


>[!VIDEO](https://video.tv.adobe.com/v/3486517/?quality=12&learn=on&enablevpops=1)

## Key takeaways

* **Job Roles Structure Project Tasks:** Job roles represent skills needed for tasks and can be assigned to users based on their abilities and availability.
* **Rate Cards Define Billing Rates:** Rate cards list billing rates for job roles and act as contracts with customers for project billing.
* **Flexible Rate Management:** Both job roles and rate cards allow setting cost and billing rates with effective dates, supporting time-based pricing changes.
* **Rate Card Overrides:** When a rate card is attached to a project, its rates override job role rates, but project-specific overrides remain unaffected.
* **Easy Attachment and Updates:** Rate cards can be attached or removed from projects, and updates can be pushed to existing projects to ensure accurate billing.
